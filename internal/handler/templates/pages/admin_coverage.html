Coverage limits

The most people of one department that may be away on any single day, as a percentage of the department. A request that would go over it is refused unless the person asks for special permission, which then needs every level of approval up to the final approver. Only leave types marked as blockable are refused; the others still count as being away. Leave a limit empty for no limit.
